Educational Context

About DECO MINI

Designed specifically for second-year undergraduate engineering students in Digital Electronics, Computer Architecture, and Microprocessor Systems.

What is Combinational Logic?

Unlike sequential logic which depends on memory elements and clock cycles, combinational logic circuits produce outputs that depend purely on the instantaneous values of their present inputs. They form the foundational building blocks of arithmetic logic units (ALUs), memory addressing units, and communication multiplexers.

Why It Matters

Understanding how TTL chips like the 74153, 74138, and 74148 operate at the pin level is critical for bridging high-level digital design and real hardware behavior. Practical engineering requires knowing how active-low strobes prevent bus contention and how priority resolvers handle simultaneous interrupt requests.

What Students Learn

  • Pinout and truth table correlation for standard 74xx ICs
  • Active-low vs. active-high signal conventions and inverted outputs
  • Multi-line enable gating and chip cascade strategies
  • Boolean expression derivation and logic simplification
